ENGINEERING CAPSTONE PROJECT

Formula IoT Racing Simulator

Application of IoT in Motorsport: Real-Time Race Car Telemetry Monitoring & Engineering Analytics

PROJECT OVERVIEW: This engineering demonstrator integrates a full 3D Formula motorsport vehicle dynamics simulator with a professional pitwall telemetry and race engineering analytics suite. The system continuously samples vehicle kinematics, powertrain thermals, aerodynamic forces, and tire degradation at 20 Hz, streaming packets through an IoT pipeline for real-time fault detection and post-race analysis.
